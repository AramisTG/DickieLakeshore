💕 Everyone, meet Kendall

One little girl. One big goal.

Kendall is 11 years old and turns 12 this February. Behind her beautiful smile is a young girl who faces challenges most of us will never fully understand.

Kendall lives with cerebral palsy and epilepsy. She is non-verbal, non-mobile, and relies on round-the-clock care from her family and caregivers.

Think about the last time you hopped in the car for a coffee, a family visit, or a fun day out. For Kendall and her family, everyday trips can be an enormous challenge.

Her family needs a wheelchair-accessible van so Kendall can travel safely to appointments, enjoy outings, and experience more of the world around her.

That's why Talkin' Funny is bringing the community together for a very special night.

❤️🚐

Every ticket brings Kendall's family closer to greater freedom and accessibility.

It's more than a night out. It's a community showing up for one another.
